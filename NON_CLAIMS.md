# NON_CLAIMS — Real-Estate Deal Sieve

This tool is a **structural multi-objective ranking filter**.

It does **not**:

- Provide investment advice or recommendations to buy, sell, or hold any property.
- Predict future cash flows, occupancy, appreciation, or exit values.
- Constitute a formal valuation, appraisal, or underwriting model.
- Guarantee that non-dominated deals will outperform dominated ones in realized returns.
- Perform portfolio construction, capital allocation, or risk budgeting.
- Use non-reducible internal state (χ) or any reconstructive-memory mechanism.
- Claim statistical significance, market efficiency, or alpha generation.

The ranking isolates deals that are not weakly dominated on the *user-supplied* objective axes after simple normalization. Any “stress” view is a deterministic re-mapping of the same input features under user-chosen rate/vacancy/exit assumptions — not a Monte-Carlo forecast.

`promote_ready = false` for any stronger commercial claim.

Users remain solely responsible for their investment decisions. Past structural ranking does not predict future performance.

EXTERNAL-clean. No-χ. Honesty primary.

# Real-Estate Deal Sieve — Concierge Offer

**Manual ranking service for real-estate and private-market deals.**

Send your deal spreadsheet. Receive a clean ranked list of non-dominated deals + basic stress view.

## What you get

1. Ranked table of your deals ordered by weak-dominance layers (rank-1 = not dominated on the objectives you care about).
2. Clear flag of which deals sit on the non-dominated frontier.
3. Simple deterministic stress view (rate / vacancy / exit-multiple pressure applied to the risk axis).
4. Short explanation of which deals dominate others under the chosen profile.

## How it works

1. Email or message your CSV (or share a private Google Sheet).
2. Tell us which profile you want:
   - Cash-flow focused (cap rate / cash-on-cash vs risk)

   - Stress-resistant
   - Or name the two columns you care about most
3. We run the sieve and return ranked CSV + short notes.

## Honesty

This is a **structural multi-objective ranking filter**.

- It does **not** predict returns, occupancy, or exit values.
- It does **not** constitute investment advice, appraisal, or underwriting.
- Non-dominated deals are simply not dominated on the axes you supplied.
- Stress numbers are a deterministic re-mapping of your inputs, not a forecast.
